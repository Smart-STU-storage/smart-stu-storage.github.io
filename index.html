Manual counting drops to zero

A load cell under every bin turns total weight into an exact piece count. Every grab and every return updates the stock the moment it happens, so reordering starts before a part runs out and a missing-screw surprise never reaches the assembly line.

How a shift with it looks

01

Set up a bin in five seconds

Press Tare, drop in exactly ten pieces, press «10 PCS». The bin now knows its part; identity and calibration live on the tray's NFC tag, so trays survive any reshuffle.

02

Just work

Take parts, put parts back. Displays on every bin show live counts, and the app shows the whole wall, straight into 1C.

03

Pick by BOM

Choose an assembly in the app. The right bins light up and show how many to take; a wrong quantity is flagged the moment it happens, not at month-end.

Scaling is adding another block

Start with one block of four weighing bins. The next block attaches on any side, top or lateral, through spring-loaded contacts, and shows up in the app on its own. Four bins on a workbench and forty on a production wall run as one system, same modules, zero configuration.
